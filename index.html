Privacy Policy

Escape 60 — mobile and web game

Effective date: 29 September 2026  Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile and web game Escape 60 (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The App is a single-player arcade game designed to be suitable for a general audience, including children.

In short: Escape 60 does not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your game progress is stored only on your own device. The App contains no chat, no messaging and no user-generated content. The App shows advertisements, and our advertising partner may collect limited device information as described below.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

None. The App has no sign-up, no login and no user accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, address, photos, contacts or location.

Information stored on your device

Your progress is saved locally on your device (levels completed, stars, best times, coins, characters, items, missions, achievements, daily rewards and your settings). This data stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us and we cannot read it.

The save file also contains a randomly generated identifier (for example 4b57105984a5bb1e53a32c8163e4881a) used only to keep your in-game content consistent on that device. It is not linked to you, your device identifiers or any other service, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2. How we use information

  • To save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • To show advertisements, which keep the game free to play.
  • To deliver items you have purchased or earned, and to prevent duplicate rewards.
  • To find and fix crashes and gameplay problems.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use information for personalised advertising directed at children.

3. Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising. Ads may include skippable video ads (also called rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward such as extra time, bonus coins or a power-up) and interstitial ads shown between levels. Watching an ad is always optional for rewards, and ads never appear during gameplay.

Advertising is provided by Google (Google AdMob and/or Google H5 Games Ads). Google may process limited device information to serve and measure these ads. Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob apps

Because the App is suitable for children, we instruct our advertising partner to treat all advertising requests as child-directed and to serve non-personalised ads only. This means ads are not based on a profile of your interests or browsing history. Ad content is restricted to family-appropriate categories.

If you buy the “Remove Ads” option, interstitial ads are permanently switched off. Optional skippable video ads remain available if you choose to watch one for a reward.

4. Children's privacy

We designed Escape 60 to be safe for children. The App:

  • does not require an account and does not collect personal information;
  • has no chat, messaging, friend lists, social features or user-generated content;
  • contains no gambling mechanics and no loot boxes;
  • requests non-personalised, child-directed advertising only;
  • shows a “please ask a grown-up” confirmation before any real-money purchase.

We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families policy and Apple's requirements for apps for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

5. In-app purchases

The App may offer optional purchases (for example coin packs, cosmetic bundles or “Remove Ads”). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card details, billing address or other payment information. Everything sold for real money can also be earned by playing.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade information. Information is only shared with the service providers needed to run the App: our advertising partner (Google) and the app store that processes purchases. We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress and settings are stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. Uninstalling the App deletes this data. You can also clear it at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Escape 60 → Storage → Clear data; iOS: delete and reinstall the App). Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, there is no account for us to delete. For any data-related request, contact us at the address below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to its processing. As the App does not collect personal data that identifies you, we usually hold nothing to provide or delete. For questions about advertising data, please refer to Google's policies linked in section 3, or contact us and we will help.

9. Device permissions

The App uses an internet connection for advertising and optional updates, and vibration for haptic feedback (which you can turn off in Settings). It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

10. Security

Game data is stored using the standard secure storage provided by your device's operating system. No passwords or payment credentials are stored by the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep the amount of data involved to an absolute minimum.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Significant changes will also be described in the app store release notes.
